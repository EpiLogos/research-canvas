## 3. EPISODE SPECIFICATION STANDARDS

### 3a. The Cardinal Rule: Readings Are Script, Everything Else Is Compass

Episode specs provide:
- **Full, untruncated Book passages** for direct reading — these are the scripted moments
- **Directional pointers** between readings — tight bullets indicating territory, register, and what the viewer needs to have grasped before the next reading lands

**The spec is a map of the emotional and intellectual arc, not a teleprompter.**

### 3b. Pointer Format

Each movement between readings gets **3–5 bullet points maximum.** Each bullet is:
- One sharp sentence or phrase indicating *what to cover* or *what register to hit*
- If a bullet needs more than two lines, it has become a script and must be cut
- **Include:** relevant etymological chains, word clusters, contrasts, and conceptual progressions — these are the speaker's primary memory hooks

**Example of GOOD pointers:**
```
- The I-Deal: ego = ideal mistaken for real. Trade soul for persona, 
  wholeness for instrumentality. The blues crossroads is literal.
- I-Deal → idol → idle → ideal: the chain of the false-sacred
- The gain: instrumentality of the model. The cost: "you become but 
  a useful part"
- Personal ↔ collective: same architecture scales. The individual 
  I-Deal IS the civilisational trade to fiat currency.
```

**Example of BAD pointers (= scripting, prohibited):**
```
- Here you should explain to the viewer that the I-Deal is the Book's 
  most technically precise concept, describing how the ego forms as a 
  kind of Faustian bargain in which the soul, rooted in the real, is 
  traded for the persona, rooted in the ideal. You could mention the 
  blues folklore about the crossroads and note that this isn't a myth 
  about the Devil but a description of ego-formation itself. Then 
  transition into how this scales from personal to collective...
```

### 3c. Etymological Chains, Word Clusters, and Conceptual Progressions

These are to be included in bullets as **compact memory hooks.** Format:

**Etymological chains** — trace a word to its root and show what it reveals:
```
- credere (to believe/trust) → credit → incredible → the only 
  power in modern currency is common belief
```

**Word clusters** — gather cognates that illuminate a single mechanism:
```
- spec- (to see): spectacle / spectre / speculation / inspect / 
  respect / suspect / expect — the whole seeing-cluster carries 
  both genuine insight and perverted surveillance
```

**Contrasts** — pair terms that define each other by opposition:
```
- non-dual (Christ) vs. un-dual (Antichrist): one reconciles 
  opposites, the other severs the marriage while remaining bound 
  to the wreckage
```

**Conceptual progressions** — track an idea through its stages:
```
- shamanic magic → mythic cosmology → rational calculative thinking 
  → integral alignment: same impulse (will to manage cosmos from 
  egoic position), progressively refined, each stage carrying the 
  shard of light further into matter
```

### 3d. Passage Placement Is Dramaturgical

Readings do the heavy lifting. Between them, pointers tell the speaker:
- **Why** this reading comes next (what it answers or opens)
- **What register** to hit approaching it (intimate? diagnostic? fierce? tender?)
- **What the viewer needs to have grasped** before the next reading can land

## 4. CORE PRINCIPLES FOR ALL EPISODES

### 4a. Register Management
Three registers: ontological (the archetype), historical-cultural (the social machinery), empirical (documented reality). No register dominates for more than ~3 minutes without the others pulling it back. The oscillation between registers IS the method — it prevents gimbal lock.

### 4b. Lead with the Image of a Being
Characterological passages from the Book present the Devil as a felt, sympathetic figure. The viewer encounters a being before encountering a concept. Abstract exposition follows and enriches the image, never replaces it.

### 4c. Direct Reading from the Book
Key passages read aloud, slowly, with commentary woven between sections. The Book's language is dense but musical — the spoken delivery honours its rhythm. Passages breathe. Silence between readings is not dead air; it is the Chora.

### 4d. Personal Coda
Each episode closes with personal reflection — a moment from the retreat, from life, from practice — that grounds the archetypal material in lived experience. Testimony, not performance. Vulnerability without exhibitionism. The holographic principle: whole in the part, part in the whole.

### 4e. Enantiodromic Lens
The shadow hides the light as much as the darkness. Every passage about the Devil's pathology is simultaneously a passage about divine capacity in exile. The viewer finishes each episode having found more light, not more darkness.

### 4f. Openness to Research
Historical sections remain open for incoming material. Specs flag where new research integrates without disrupting existing architecture.

### 4g. Visual Language
Evocative, layered, suggestive rather than illustrative. Avoid literalism. Recurring motifs: chessboard (duality/the game), volcano (compressed fire), chain (bondage/connection), baby (innocence/futurity), hermit's hood (concealment/contemplation).

### 4h. Language and Neologism
Familiar terms used in unfamiliar ways (currency, mesmerisation). Neologisms coined when existing language collapses into dualism (un-finite, un-dual, I-Deal, Promise Land, mono-poly). Each coinage should feel like relief — "oh, THAT's the word for it."

### 4i. Paradox as Signature
Wherever the material arrives at paradox, that signals proximity to truth. Do not resolve. Hold. The symbolic method exists to make this bearable and generative.

### 4j. Compassion as Ground
"Loving sensitivity to origins." Not sentimentality. The most demanding form of attention. Understanding HOW things come to be what they are — including the most horrifying things — without excusing or demonising.

## 6. CAUTIONS AND FRAMINGS

### 6a. Treatment of Traditions
We are committed to "everything is everything" (Shaivite ground). Because all is sacred, nothing is sacred insofar as ideas are concerned. We reach through ideas to their heart, which may be unrecognisable at times. This is not disrespect — it is the refusal to let a tradition become an idol.

### 6b. The Subject-Object Split as Historical Product
The lost parity between sciences of the inner (contemplative, psycho-spiritual, "irrational") and sciences of the outer (rational, material, measurable) is not a philosophical accident. It is historically, institutionally, deliberately maintained. The ruling class operates on a richer ontological map than it permits the rest of us. "There is no more powerless ontology than materialism" — which is precisely why materialism is propagated for the many.

### 6c. Perspective to Context
Three perspectives (triangle, 180°) = partiality. Four (square, 360°) = first contextual totality. Context is the compound of perspectives. The move from perspective to context liberates knowledge from egoic myopia. The Devil is perspective-locked (gimbal lock). QL's toroidal architecture prevents this — no orientation-dependent singularities. The quaternion analogy: you literally need 4D to avoid gimbal lock in rotation.

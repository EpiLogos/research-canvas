## MOVEMENT 2: DEFINITIONAL GROUND — WHAT IS AN ARCHETYPE?

*Spoken throughout, no readings. This is the frame you hang everything on. The poem has just told us that words reveal and re-conceal — now we show that Jung's own language did exactly this.*

### Jung's buried lead:

- Culturally we've taken up a lot of Jung's language — complexes, synchronicity, introversion and extraversion, archetypes. But by virtue of the language he used to fit within the scientific materialism of his times — the only way he could publish (hence requesting the Red Book be published posthumously) — the lead was buried. The lead being: the necessary metaphysical view that would support his oeuvre.
- Bernardo Kastrup delves into Jung's metaphysics and develops it into analytic idealism — the view that reality is fundamentally experiential. In time, Kastrup found that this metaphysics is essentially identical to the Kashmir Shaivite view, which he explored through his working friendship with Rupert Spira. The Eastern tradition that takes consciousness as primary reality and the Western depth-psychological tradition that had to disguise this insight converge on the same topology. Con-spirare — breathing together.

### Why the archetype-as-such/image distinction couldn't enter common parlance:

- This distinction depends upon a metaphysics of above and below — a view that gives credence to both a shared phenomenal reality AND nested layers of reality, all of which are the work of a dreaming deity, an infinite intelligence, using high-dimensional patterns of organisation to generate inherently interconnected images/manifestations that exist contemporaneous to time in a relatively timeless realm.
- Under materialism, there's nothing above the phenomenon. The phenomenon is all there is. So all you can talk about are archetypal images — patterns in the data, recurring motifs — without ever asking what GENERATES them.

### Psychic facticity — the neutral ground:

- Jung doesn't say "consciousness is ultimate reality" — that would be metaphysics, and he'd be dismissed. He says "the psyche produces facts." This is a neutral claim. It doesn't require you to be an idealist or a materialist. It just asks you to accord inner experience the same epistemic weight you give outer experience. The dream is as real an EVENT as the sunrise — whatever its ontological status, it HAPPENED, it carries information. Jung: "I don't believe — I know."
- Deleuze (*Difference and Repetition*) provides the philosophical warrant: genuine thinking is not a voluntary exercise. It begins with an involuntary encounter — something that FORCES thought, that exceeds all existing categories. True thought is a spiritual ordeal (*épreuve spirituelle*). Inner confrontation is the primary site of knowledge production.

### The distinction itself — sun and solar system:

- The archetype-as-such (*an sich*): irrepresentable, imageless, the pattern that generates images. Like the sun — everything in the solar system is the sun in some essential sense. Every planet, ocean, leaf, creature is solar energy transformed, stored, expressed. But in its forms it's utterly unrecognisable as sunlight. A redwood tree IS the sun, but it looks nothing like the sun.
- The archetypal image: what we actually encounter — in dreams, visions, myths, cultures, art. The orbit, the trajectory, the specific manifestation. Each image traces the attractor without being the attractor.
- Van Eenwyk (*Archetypes and Strange Attractors*, 1997): archetypes function like strange attractors in nonlinear dynamics. Never reached, never fully instantiated — but they shape every trajectory in their basin. Self-similar at every scale (fractal) but never identical. The symbol is the basin of attraction.

### The spectral / harmonic principle:

- Archetypes do not exist in isolation. They exist along a continuum — a spectrum — with one another. When you invoke one, its resonances come with it, like overtones of a fundamental note. The Devil-as-Magician brings the con-man, the priest, the doctor, the shape-shifter. These aren't different archetypes — they're harmonics of the same fundamental. The symbol is a CHORD, not a note.

### The psychoid — first hint:

- Pauli and Jung's deeper project: the psychoid nature of number. Number sits at the intersection of inner and outer — simultaneously subjective (numinous, meaningful) and objective (mathematical, invariant). Archetypes-as-such sit at this psychoid level — beneath the psyche/physis split. They generate BOTH psychological images AND physical structures. Form-generating principles that are simultaneously rigorous and creative. This thread deepens across the series.

### The Self-Christ-Antichrist-Logos chain:

- Jung identified Christ as the primary Western symbol of the archetype of the Self — the Self being the archetype of WHOLENESS, the totality of the psyche including conscious and unconscious.
- If Christ symbolises the Self, then the Antichrist symbolises the Self's SHADOW — everything the conscious identification with "Christ" (light, goodness, perfection) has to exclude to maintain its one-sidedness.
- The Self is also connected to the Logos — *logos* (Greek): word, reason, ordering intelligence, the principle that gives meaning and structure to reality. The Self as Logos means: the archetype of wholeness IS the ordering, meaning-giving intelligence of reality itself.
- The Antichrist is the ANTI-Logos — the corruption and fragmentation of meaning-giving intelligence. Not the absence of meaning but its PERVERSION — meaning turned against itself, the Word that lies, the order that disorders.
- This is where the whole series lives. The Antichrist is the shadow of meaning itself.

But the proposed solution — return to ONE true framework as the only possible ground for knowledge — is itself a symptom of the very dynamic it diagnoses. ## MOVEMENT 8: CLOSE — THE NAKED FACE

### READ — "Who Am I?" (line 64):

> How is it we could overturn this situation and initiate ourselves out of his world? I say that we must come to wonder who we are, and buy into no answer the Devil tries to sell us. We must learn to watch the Devil at work in the shadows of our need, and learn to need him not. Let the heart earnestly and diligently wonder "Who am I?". The Devil has a great deal of care in his shrivelled heart, for he, like us, wants to know who he is. But his deepest confusion is in believing that "wanting to know who he is" is equivalent to "just wanting to be me". Because he is more than he can let himself conceive or accept of himself, "me" will never amount to "his" Self. This is true for us too — what we are in our deepest Being goes far beyond what we could ever think of ourselves — settling for "me" cuts out our deeper identity as "that which Is", the unqualified "I AM". The heart already knows how to answer the question "who am I?" — with silent Presence, and indeed has already answered. Language and thought, it knows, have no purchase here.

### PERSONAL CODA (spoken, brief — not full readings):

- Everything I've just described, I discovered first in myself, on retreat. In a single night of inner confrontation — the kind Deleuze calls a spiritual ordeal — the archetype-as-such and its image revealed themselves not as theory but as the structure of my own psyche. The "I" with no image, the I-image that fabricates, the awakened shadow that thinks it's already awake — I met all of this in the space of a few hours.
- The shattering moment: when the I-image said it acted the way it did "because it wanted to be me" — and I realised that the "me" it was referring to had no image, was no one, was completely anonymous. The imago trying to become the archetype-as-such and failing. The image orbiting the attractor, endlessly. That WAS the looping — the catch-22 everyone recognises from their own experience.
- What's distinctive about this confrontation — and what separates it from both classical Advaita witnessing and standard Jungian shadow-work — is that the Self doesn't merely witness, and doesn't merely analyse. It turns and ADDRESSES the ego-structure directly, and the structure speaks back. The I-image becomes an interlocutor with its own motives, its own confession: "because I don't feel real." This is the face-to-face encounter. Neither passive witnessing nor external analysis — a direct confrontation of Self with unconscious ego-structure as object.
- The theory isn't theory. It's the fruit of an encounter. And the encounter is available to anyone willing to crack the shell of their own looping and look at what's inside.

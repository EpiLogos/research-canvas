# Episode Tranche 1 (Episodes 1.0–1.5)

## What Happened

The original plan was a single cycle of episodes numbered 0.0–0.5, each at a QL position. Episode 0.0 ("The Threshold") was recorded. Episode 0.1 ("The Naked Face") was scripted to v9 and recorded — but in delivery, the episode ran to about an hour and only got through roughly 60-70% of the script, up to partway through what was planned as Movement 2 (the definitional ground: the solar system image, psychic facticity, the spectral principle, the as-such/image distinction, the mono-poly, the need dynamic, and the beginning of the QL units).

The planned QL unit introductions, the Devil's sixfold spectral lineage in full, the Book's portraits, the architecture of power, the language section, and the quilting return — all of this was scripted but never reached in the recording. This is fine: the content is strong, there's plenty for multiple episodes, and the format works at ~60 minutes per episode.

## The Renumbering

What was episode 0.1 is now **episode 1.0** — the first episode of Tranche 1. The remaining scripted material from the v9 spec, plus the substantial conceptual developments that emerged in the ep-1.1 handover conversation, now distributes across episodes **1.1 through 1.5**.

The original episode 0.2 ("The Fire of the Gods") was a separate spec focused on the historical throughline (Medici, Nazi-OSS-CIA, Epstein). That material is now in Tranche 2 (`/episodes/2/`).

## What 1.0 Established (What the Viewer Already Has)

From the recorded delivery of "The Naked Face":

- **The solar system as image** — everything in the system IS the sun, the source gives itself completely into forms that no longer resemble it
- **Psychic facticity** — the bare fact of awareness, attending to the phenomenon as given, Jung's "the psyche produces facts"
- **The as-such / image distinction** — the archetype-as-such is irrepresentable, the images orbit it like planets orbit the sun
- **The spectral/harmonic principle** — archetypes arrive as chords, white light through a prism, van Eenwyk's strange attractors
- **The mono-poly** — monotheism = the sun, polytheism = the solar system, mono-poly-theism = the recognition that these are ONE
- **Need** — the sun meets needs freely, the black sun captures needs and sells them back
- **Kepler** — the music of the spheres, the split between sacred and mechanical cosmology
- **The quilting method** — point de capiton, each pass enriching the previous retroactively

## What 1.0 Did NOT Get To (What Lands in 1.1+)

These were scripted in v9 but not reached in the recording:

- The three QL units formally named and diagrammed

- The quilting return and personal coda
- The Self-Christ-Antichrist-Logos chain

## What Developed AFTER 1.0 (New for 1.1)

The ep-1.1 handover conversation (`ep-1.1/handover/seed.md`) generated major conceptual developments:

- **Self-Identity as parent node** — the non-dual root named, the inversion of inversion, the four prepositions, the copula chain
- **Christ's spectral sixfold** — God → Zarathustra → Prometheus → Christ → Jesus → God-Man (was "held for 0.2" in the v9 spec)
- **The double helix topology** — Devil's chain and Christ's chain as two strands of one DNA, Prometheus at #2 as the base pair, convergence at #5
- **The sun/clouded sky reframing** — the sun never goes black, the sky does; evil operates in the medium, not at the source
- **The deficiency unit** — each ontological position in health and pathology, the cascade logic
- **The power unit as "the other side" of Self-Identity** — the game of "what am I"
- **The numerical architecture** — 0-5 via Pythagoras and Jung, the perfect number, the genus topology
- **QL formally named** via the Jung-Pauli psychoid bridge
- **The explication structure** — parent node → 5/0 framing → inner four (1-4)

## Key Principle: The Poem

Every episode in Tranche 1 opens with the poem from the Book:

> *Words reveal and re-conceal the real — they make up the makeup that adorns the face of the world, and at best accentuate and enhance the natural contours of its beauty, at worst contort it into a clownish facade that doesn't feel or breathe — words when cleared from the pores reveal the sheet beneath the score — words reapplied where cleanliness is denied weave a bandage over sores — re-presenting for disguise, naked bare with no surprise, realise the seer dies when faced with mind's reflected sight*

The poem IS the series in miniature. It enacts the dynamic it describes. Each episode passes through it again, and each time it means more.

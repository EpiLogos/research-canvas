# Banking dynasties, royal bloodlines, and the money behind the wars

**The five-century arc from Medici Florence to the world wars reveals a documented pattern — not of shadowy puppet-masters, but of something arguably more disturbing: structural features of international capitalism that repeatedly produce cross-belligerent financing as a natural byproduct.** Medici blood entered virtually every European royal house through two queens of France, creating a genetic web that connected Florentine banking to the thrones of Spain, Austria, Britain, and Savoy. The Rothschilds built a more resilient multinational network a century after the Medici bank collapsed, converting financial power into political influence through methods the Medici pioneered. And between 1933 and 1945, documented evidence from congressional investigations, Nuremberg trials, and declassified archives reveals that Anglo-American capital flowed to Nazi Germany through corporate cartels, banking relationships, and institutional mechanisms — though the evidence consistently points to profit-seeking and structural inertia rather than deliberate grand strategy. The crucial distinction this report maintains throughout: **documented fact, well-evidenced inference, and pattern recognition are explicitly separated**, because this territory has been so thoroughly colonized by conspiracy theorists that the genuinely alarming documented record is often obscured by mythology.

## The Medici dispersal: how banking blood entered every throne room in Europe

The Medici family's genetic penetration of European royalty operated through two principal vectors, both queens of France. **Catherine de' Medici** married Henry II in 1533, a match arranged by her uncle Pope Clement VII. Three of her sons became kings of France (Francis II, Charles IX, Henry III), but none produced surviving legitimate heirs — the Valois dynasty died with them. The lasting dynastic impact came through her daughters: Elisabeth married Philip II of Spain, and through her daughter Catherine Michelle, Medici blood entered the House of Savoy, which eventually provided the kings of unified Italy. More consequentially, Catherine's daughter Claude married into the House of Lorraine and produced Christina of Lorraine, who married back into the Medici family — creating a genealogical loop that would matter greatly in 1737.

**Marie de' Medici**, who married Henry IV of France in 1600, proved the more consequential dynastic vector. The marriage was fundamentally a debt settlement: Henry owed the Medici **1,174,000 écus**, and Marie's dowry of 600,000 écus was structured to cancel this debt, earning her the nickname *"la grosse banquière."* Her son Louis XIII fathered Louis XIV, through whom **all subsequent Bourbons descend** — the kings of France, Spain (including the current King Felipe VI), Naples-Sicily, and Parma. Her daughter Henrietta Maria married Charles I of England, making Marie de' Medici the direct ancestor of Charles II, James II, and through them, the current British royal family. Prince William and Prince Harry carry Medici blood through multiple lines of descent.

The Medici-Habsburg connection was built through a series of calculated marriages spanning two centuries, but its financial architecture was simple: **money bought legitimacy**. The Medici were originally bourgeois bankers, not nobility. Cosimo I paid **100,000 ducats** for Imperial recognition of his Grand Ducal title. Medici dowries functioned as geopolitical instruments. The Rome branch of the Medici bank — which served as the papal depository general — accounted for roughly half the bank's total profits at its peak, creating a financial-religious nexus that three Medici popes (Leo X, Clement VII, Leo XI) exploited ruthlessly.

Leo X's pontificate is the clearest example of banking mentality applied to papal power. He **accrued 400,000 ducats in debt** while monetizing every available papal asset: selling indulgences, creating and selling 42 new cardinal positions, pawning papal jewels and even statues of the apostles. His sale of indulgences to fund St. Peter's Basilica directly precipitated the Protestant Reformation — arguably the most consequential financial miscalculation in European history. Clement VII arranged Catherine's marriage to the French heir and refused Henry VIII's annulment, triggering the English Reformation. These popes converted banking acumen into theological authority and then deployed that authority for dynastic advancement.

A critical chronological clarification: **the Medici bank failed in 1494**, fully 243 years before the dynasty expired. The bank and the dynasty are separate stories. When the male Medici line died with Gian Gastone in 1737, his sister Anna Maria Luisa preserved the art collection through the *Patto di Famiglia*, but no banking networks remained to transfer. The Fuggers of Augsburg had already replaced the Medici as Europe's dominant banking dynasty in the sixteenth century, financing Habsburg wars with loans that dwarfed anything the Medici managed. The Rothschilds emerged two centuries later with no institutional continuity to either house — though they independently reinvented several Medici structural innovations.

## From Frankfurt's ghetto to Wellington's paymaster: the Rothschild model

Mayer Amschel Rothschild was born in 1744 in the Frankfurt Judengasse — a walled ghetto where Jews were locked in at night, forbidden from parks, inns, or promenades, and confined to a house less than ten feet wide shared by two families. His ascent began through coin dealing with William IX, Landgrave of Hesse-Kassel, whose fortune derived from renting **17,000-20,000 soldiers** to Britain during the American Revolution at annual retainers of £125,000-£240,000. Niall Ferguson's archival research corrects the legend: Rothschild did not receive William's "entire fortune" during Napoleon's 1806 invasion. He managed one specific service — William's English investments — but this proved the bridge that launched Nathan Mayer Rothschild from Manchester textile merchant to London banker.

The five-son structure (Amschel in Frankfurt, Salomon in Vienna, Nathan in London, Carl in Naples, James in Paris) represented a genuine innovation over the Medici model. Where the Medici staffed branches with hired managers who sometimes embezzled or made unauthorized loans — the London branch's disastrous lending to Edward IV cost **51,533 gold florins** — the Rothschilds solved the principal-agent problem by placing blood relatives in every key city. Ferguson describes the houses as working "so closely that it is impossible to discuss the history of one without discussing the history of all five."

**Nathan's financing of Wellington is extensively documented.** From 1811 onward, he transferred money to pay Wellington's troops in Iberia, working with Commissary-General Herries to purchase gold and silver from across the world for delivery in local currency. The British government turned to Nathan because its own networks had failed and established banks had let it down. **In 1815 alone, the Rothschilds provided £9.8 million in subsidy loans to Britain's continental allies** — approximately £850 million in 2023 terms. Nathan reportedly called it "the best business I ever did."

The famous Waterloo manipulation story — that Nathan learned of victory first via carrier pigeon and crashed the bond market to buy cheap — is **substantially debunked**. Brian Cathcart's archival research found no evidence of organized pigeon post in England in 1815. Ferguson's analysis of financial records calculated that even with advance information, available trades "could not have generated more than £7,000 in profits." The Rothschilds had actually bet on a protracted war; Napoleon's sudden defeat disrupted their plans. The story originated in Georges Dairnvaell's 1846 antisemitic pamphlet, was embellished by subsequent writers, and was weaponized in the 1940 Nazi propaganda film *Die Rothschilds*.

The broader claim that the Rothschilds "financed both sides" of the Napoleonic Wars is **not supported by Ferguson's extensive archival research**. James operated in Paris, but his documented wartime role was purchasing bullion for Nathan's British operations, not lending to Napoleon. The Rothschilds became bankers to the post-Napoleonic French government from 1823 onward — post-war lending, not wartime funding of opposing belligerents. Ferguson addressed this directly: "Having made their money, they stood to lose more than they gained from conflict...The Rothschilds had decided the outcome of the Napoleonic Wars by putting their financial weight behind Britain." The "both sides" claim is a core antisemitic conspiracy theory that should be recognized as such.

The Rothschild pattern of converting financial power into political influence paralleled the Medici model but operated under a critical constraint. As Jews in an era of systemic antisemitism, the Rothschilds could not directly enter government the way the Medici occupied the papacy and ruled Florence. Lionel de Rothschild was elected to Parliament in **1847** but could not take his seat for eleven years because the oath required swearing "on the true faith of a Christian." Their influence was necessarily exercised through governments rather than as governments — through loans, intelligence, and social relationships. The Austrian baronetcy of 1822 came because Metternich himself requested it, impressed by the Rothschilds' reliability in remitting subsidy payments "without making undue profits."

## Who funded Hitler: what the documents actually show

This is the most contested territory in the research, and maintaining epistemological discipline requires distinguishing several categories: documented direct financial support for the Nazi Party, corporate relationships that incidentally benefited the regime, cartel agreements that materially harmed Allied war capacity, and commercial relationships that preexisted Nazi rule. The documented record is simultaneously more specific and more ambiguous than either conspiracy theorists or their debunkers typically acknowledge.

**Prescott Bush and the Union Banking Corporation** is the most frequently mythologized case. The documented facts: UBC was seized under the Trading with the Enemy Act on October 20, 1942 (Vesting Order No. 248). UBC was wholly owned by Bank voor Handel en Scheepvaart, a Dutch bank controlled by the Thyssen family. Prescott Bush was one of seven directors, holding one share worth $125. Fritz Thyssen's support for Hitler is independently documented — **100,000 gold marks** donated in 1923, approximately **650,000 Reichsmarks** to right-wing parties between 1923 and 1932, and organization of a **3 million Reichsmark** donation from German industrialists for the March 1933 election. Bush was a managing director at UBC and a partner at Brown Brothers Harriman, which served as UBC's private banker. After liquidation, Bush received **$1.5 million**. Historians Herbert Parmet and Joe Conason assessed Bush's involvement as "purely commercial" with no evidence of Nazi sympathy. The chain of intentionality from Bush to Hitler is indirect — but the relationship continued after substantial information about Nazi atrocities was available.

**I.G. Farben's American connections** are documented through congressional investigations totaling tens of thousands of pages. The 1929 cartel agreement with Standard Oil of New Jersey gave Standard the hydrogenation process in exchange for **546,011 shares of Standard stock** (~$35 million). The JASCO joint venture gave I.G. Farben the right to refuse licensing Buna synthetic rubber patents in the United States — and they refused. The most damning document is Standard Oil VP Frank Howard's own words about the October 1939 arrangement: "Representatives of I.G. Farbenindustrie delivered to me assignments of some 2,000 foreign patents and we did our best to work out complete plans for a modus vivendi which would operate through the term of the war **whether or not the U.S. came in**." The Truman Committee concluded Standard Oil "did hamper the development of synthetic rubber in the United States." Senator Truman called it "treason." Standard signed a consent decree in March 1942, released 2,000 patents royalty-free, and paid a **$50,000 fine**. Thurman Arnold identified **162 separate agreements** between I.G. Farben and American corporations — Standard Oil was the most prominent but far from unique.

**Henry Ford's** contribution to Nazi ideology is documented beyond serious dispute. The *Dearborn Independent* published antisemitic content from 1920 to 1927, compiled into *The International Jew*, which reached **21 printings in Germany by 1922**. At Nuremberg, Hitler Youth leader Baldur von Schirach testified: "The decisive anti-Semitic book I was reading...was that book by Henry Ford, *The International Jew*. I read it and became anti-Semitic." Hitler praised Ford in *Mein Kampf* — the only American mentioned positively — and displayed a life-size portrait of Ford in his Munich office. Ford received the Grand Cross of the German Eagle on July 30, 1938, and never returned it. Ford-Werke in Cologne used **2,000-2,500 forced laborers** at any time, including inmates from Buchenwald. However, Ford's own 2001 investigation (reviewing 98,000+ pages) found "no documentation...suggests either knowledge, acquiescence, or control by Dearborn" of forced labor — a finding contested by historian Max Wallace, who claims declassified FBI files reveal a "financial paper trail." Whether Ford provided direct cash to the NSDAP remains **genuinely contested**: Nazi fundraiser Kurt Ludecke was "apparently refused" a direct contribution in 1924, but Ford gave "considerable sums" to Boris Brasol, a member of an organization that financed the fledgling Nazi Party.

**Montagu Norman's** relationship with Hjalmar Schacht is documented through Bank of England archives. Norman was godfather to Schacht's grandson, named "Norman" in his honor. He reportedly told a Morgan partner that "Hitler and Schacht are the bulwarks of civilisation in Germany." The Czech gold transfer of March 1939 — **£5.6 million** (roughly £700 million-£1.1 billion today) moved from Czech to Reichsbank accounts on the same day as the request — was knowingly authorized by Norman, who argued it would be "wrong and dangerous for the future of the BIS" to block it for political reasons. Historian David Blaazer established through internal memos that Norman knew exactly which accounts were Czech and German, despite later evasions.

**The Schroeder/Schroder connection** links the January 4, 1933 meeting — historian Karl Dietrich Bracher's "Birth Hour of the Third Reich" — to the City of London. Baron Kurt von Schröder hosted Hitler and Papen at his Cologne villa; their agreement led to Hitler's appointment as Chancellor on January 30. Both Kurt von Schröder's Cologne bank (J.H. Stein & Co.) and J. Henry Schroder & Co. in London descend from the same Hanseatic family. Kurt was identified as a great-grandson of the same Johann Heinrich Schröder who founded the London bank. Frank Cyril Tiarks, a managing director of the London Schroders and a director of the Bank of England from 1912 to 1945, met with Nazi philosopher Alfred Rosenberg. However, the degree of operational coordination between the German and English branches on Nazi financing specifically is **not conclusively established** by mainstream scholarship.

## The pattern of cross-belligerent finance: structure, not conspiracy

The broader pattern of funding reaching both sides of conflicts between 1847 and 1947 is documented, but the evidence consistently points to structural features of international capitalism rather than deliberate grand strategy.

**During World War I**, the three surviving Rothschild houses served their respective national governments on opposite sides — London and Paris for the Allies, Vienna for Austria-Hungary. Louis von Rothschild actively supported the Austro-Hungarian war bond campaign, writing publicly that "just as the soldier in the trenches fulfils his duty to the fatherland, everyone should contribute." He received the Großkreuz des Franz-Joseph-Ordens for his contributions. His cousins served as first lieutenants in the Imperial Dragoons. But this was not a strategy — the war **devastated** the Rothschild network, shifting financial power permanently from London to New York. The Vienna house never recovered. Communication between the houses in enemy countries was severed during the war.

**ITT Corporation** represents perhaps the most extraordinary documented case. CEO Sosthenes Behn met Hitler on August 3, 1933. Through subsidiary C. Lorenz AG, ITT owned **25-29% of Focke-Wulf**, manufacturer of Luftwaffe fighters. SS General Kurt von Schröder joined ITT's board and served as a conduit for payments to Himmler's SS. As Anthony Sampson documented: "While ITT-Focke-Wulf planes were bombing Allied ships and ITT lines were passing information to German submarines, ITT direction-finders were saving other ships from torpedoes." After the war, ITT collected **$27 million** in compensation from the U.S. government for Allied bombing damage to its German factories — including **$5 million** for damage to Focke-Wulf plants. Behn received the Medal of Merit, the highest U.S. civilian award, for ITT's wartime services to the Allies. Sampson's assessment: "If the Nazis had won, ITT in Germany would have appeared impeccably Nazi; as they lost, it re-emerged as impeccably American."

**The Bank for International Settlements** operated as perhaps the most structurally significant cross-belligerent institution. Its wartime board included convicted war criminals: **Walther Funk**, **Hermann Schmitz** (I.G. Farben), **Baron von Schröder**, and **Emil Puhl**, who referred to the BIS as the Reichsbank's "only real foreign branch." At peak, the Reichsbank and occupied-country central banks held **67.4% of BIS voting stock**. The BIS received **3.7 tonnes of gold** from the Reichsbank that had been looted from Belgium and the Netherlands. American president Thomas McKittrick, re-elected in 1942 with German acquiescence, told private businessmen not to be "distracted by Nazi aggression." The 1944 Bretton Woods resolution calling for BIS liquidation was quietly set aside by 1948 — European central bankers successfully lobbied to preserve the institution.

**Swiss neutrality** functioned as the essential financial bridge. The Bergier Commission (1996-2002, producing 25 volumes and 11,000 pages) found the Swiss National Bank received approximately **$440 million** in gold from Nazi sources, of which **$316 million** was looted from occupied countries' central banks. Senior SNB officials knew as early as 1941 they were dealing in looted gold. The Eizenstat Report, based on **15 million pages** of declassified documents, described Switzerland as "Nazi Germany's banker."

Thurman Arnold's observation from 1942 captures the structural reality: "There is not a single monopoly, in any capitalist country, which does not have international cartel agreements, and which is not attempting to continue these agreements despite the war." He identified 162 separate agreements between I.G. Farben alone and American corporations. A 1974 Senate Judiciary Committee report stated that GM and Ford, "due to their multinational dominance of motor vehicle production...became principal suppliers for the forces of fascism as well as for the forces of democracy."

## The British establishment's German entanglement

The British aristocracy's German connections ran deep before any question of Nazism arose. Queen Victoria married her first cousin Prince Albert of Saxe-Coburg-Gotha, and their descendants occupied thrones across Europe. By World War I, two of Victoria's grandsons (George V and Kaiser Wilhelm II) and a grandson-in-law (Tsar Nicholas II) led three of the warring nations. The 1917 renaming to "Windsor" was prompted by anti-German sentiment intensified by Gotha bomber raids on London. Historian Karina Urbach documented through 30 archives worldwide that by the 1930s, "at least half of [Britain's] aristocracy possessed German relatives."

The **Anglo-German Fellowship** (founded September 1935) was not a fringe organization. Its membership of ~900 included **three directors of the Bank of England**, approximately 50 MPs from both Houses, and representatives from every stratum of the establishment: Lord Londonderry, the Duke of Wellington, Lord Nuffield, the Duke of Westminster. Frank Cyril Tiarks — simultaneously a Bank of England director and managing director of J. Henry Schroder — was a member. They hosted grand dinners at London's finest hotels for Rudolf Hess, Ribbentrop, and General von Blomberg. Charles Spicer's 2018 PhD thesis argues the Fellowship "has been consequently misinterpreted and underestimated" and achieved "international credibility as a lobbying body, diplomatic intermediary and intelligence-gathering tool."

**Lord Rothermere** and the *Daily Mail* exemplify how media power intersected with pro-fascist sentiment. The "Hurrah for the Blackshirts" editorial of January 1934 urged young men to join Mosley's BUF. Rothermere's other papers offered free BUF rally tickets and ran "Why I Like the Blackshirts" competitions. His personal correspondence with Hitler — intercepted by MI6 — included writing to the Führer: "Frederick the Great was a great popular figure. I salute your excellency's star which rises higher and higher." MI5 files record that intelligence services warned the government that publication of this correspondence "would shock the British public." Rothermere broke with Mosley after six months, but his admiration for Hitler persisted past Kristallnacht. He died in exile in Bermuda in 1940, fearing treason charges.

The **Duke of Windsor** case is the most extensively documented through multiple archives. Spanish diplomatic records discovered by Karina Urbach show that in June 1940, the Duke told a Spanish diplomat he blamed "the Jews, the Reds and the Foreign Office for the war" and stated that "if one bombed England effectively this could bring peace." The Marburg Files — ~60 captured German documents published in 1957 over Churchill's objections — reveal a German ambassador's cable that the Duke "characterizes himself as a firm supporter of a peaceful arrangement with Germany." Operation Willi, authorized by Hitler with a budget of **55 million Reichsmarks**, aimed to kidnap or lure the Duke for use as a puppet king. Churchill appointed him Governor of the Bahamas specifically to remove him from German reach, threatening court-martial if he refused. FBI files — memos to President Roosevelt — warned that "the Duchess of Windsor was exceedingly pro-German in her sympathies."

Richard Griffiths' landmark 1980 study *Fellow Travellers of the Right* established that pro-Nazi sentiment "affected a remarkably wide area of society" and was not confined to extremist groups. **Anti-communism was the primary driver.** Lord Londonderry explicitly advocated Anglo-German cooperation to harness Germany's "ideological opposition to Marxism," while Lord Halifax acknowledged to the Germans what "Hitler had done for Germany, especially eliminating Communism." The peak of pro-German sentiment was surprisingly late — **1936-1937** — after which it gradually declined until, by the outbreak of war, "it was confined to extremist groups and isolated outcrops." The Nazis themselves miscalculated: Ribbentrop believed the aristocracy controlled Britain. Rudolf Hess's 1941 flight to Scotland was, as historians note, symptomatic of this fundamental misreading of where power actually resided in British society.

## Conclusion: what the documented record reveals and where trails go cold

The five-century arc from Medici Florence to the aftermath of World War II reveals three distinct phenomena that are frequently conflated but should be separated.

**First, dynastic-financial architecture is real and documented.** The Medici converted banking profits into papal power, sovereign titles, and marriage alliances that placed their blood in every major European royal house. The Rothschilds independently reinvented key elements of this model — multinational family networks, conversion of financial indispensability into political access — while operating under constraints the Medici never faced. These are documented facts, not conspiracy theories. The Medici innovation of using a holding-company structure for international banking, and the Rothschild innovation of deploying family members rather than hired managers to staff foreign branches, represent genuine milestones in the architecture of financial power. However, direct institutional continuity between these houses does not exist: the Medici bank collapsed in 1494, the Fuggers replaced them, and the Rothschilds emerged three centuries later from entirely independent origins.

**Second, cross-belligerent financing during the world wars is documented through primary sources — but the evidence points overwhelmingly to structural features of international capitalism rather than coordinated strategy.** The Standard Oil-I.G. Farben modus vivendi of October 1939, ITT's simultaneous service to both sides, the BIS's transfer of Czech gold, and Swiss banking's laundering of looted gold are all documented through congressional investigations, Nuremberg trials, government commissions, and corporate archives. But Thurman Arnold's 1942 observation remains the most honest interpretation: these were not aberrations but the natural consequence of international monopoly capitalism encountering national belligerency. The institutional frameworks — patent law, banking secrecy, BIS protocols — that enabled cross-belligerent finance reflected structural tensions inherent in a globalized financial system, not conspiratorial design.

**Third, the documentation deliberately thins at certain critical junctures, and this thinning is itself significant.** The Royal Archives remain closed regarding Windsor correspondence with German relatives. Churchill pressured Eisenhower to suppress the Marburg Files for decades. The War Department intervened to prevent full prosecution of Standard Oil executives. The Bretton Woods resolution to dissolve the BIS was quietly abandoned. Norman's evasions about the Czech gold were accepted. These are not gaps in the historical record — they are documented decisions to limit the historical record, and they mark the boundary where documented fact yields to well-evidenced inference.

The most honest conclusion the evidence supports is not that a secret cabal funded both sides of wars as a grand strategy. It is something more structurally disturbing: **that international finance, by its nature, creates cross-belligerent connections that persist through wars, that legal and institutional frameworks protect these connections, and that the individuals who benefit from them have consistently used their influence to prevent full documentation and accountability.** The money did not flow to both sides because puppet-masters pulled strings. It flowed to both sides because money, unimpeded, flows everywhere — and the people positioned at its nodes have always had the power to ensure that this structural reality remains poorly understood.

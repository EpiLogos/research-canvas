# Research Canvas

Local-first tool for epistemic and historical ideation and agentic knowledge work: one product, many surfaces over one graph. The Antichrist series is its bootstrapping profile, not its whole reason for being.

## The product

**Surface**:
A way of seeing the same graph — canvas, timeline, psychogeographic (map/street/globe), story, mind palace.
_Avoid_: lens, mode

**Substrate**:
The domain-neutral core of the model: locked categories, relationships, temporal validity, place, provenance, and open QL tagging. Never gains profile-specific vocabulary.
_Avoid_: base ontology

**Profile**:
A domain overlay that shapes how timelines and constellations are composed and read. Antichrist/Epi-Logos is the bootstrapping profile; migration is the second profile.
_Avoid_: project, domain pack, mode

**Bootstrapping profile**:
The profile in which the QL field itself is derived, explained, and made available — Antichrist/Epi-Logos.
_Avoid_: source profile

**QL-open tagging**:
Optional archetypal fields on the substrate — never required, never first-class in visible surfaces.
_Avoid_: archetypal tagging, QL metadata

## The model

**Temporal Place**:
A place with coordinates at an explicit precision, a hierarchy, and time-bounded identity.
_Avoid_: pin, location, point

**Sub-timeline**:
A timeline framed by a node, nesting inside other timelines; the main timeline's spatial zero-case is Earth.
_Avoid_: nested view, filtered timeline

**Zero-case**:
The outermost frame of the main timeline — Earth spatially. Trans-temporal nodes hover above all timelines rather than nesting inside them.
_Avoid_: root timeline

**Scene**:
A profile-level unit joining a place frame, a time window, the people involved, and the media/voice passages anchored there. Walks and stories are sequences of scenes.
_Avoid_: chapter, moment, step

## The pipeline

**Source artifact**:
Any raw input — transcript, chat log, recording, video, photo, map, document — that is canonical and agent-immutable.
_Avoid_: file, note, document

**Passage-level provenance**:
Every graph object's link back to the exact passage of a source artifact at its native unit — text span, timestamp range, image region.
_Avoid_: source link, citation

**Facilitator instrument**:
The canvas as the professional's authoring tool; everyone else meets the work through presentation surfaces.
_Avoid_: dashboard, workbench

**Presentation surface**:
A published experience of a story or walk — navigable, own-language, exportable as a keepsake.
_Avoid_: export, read-layer

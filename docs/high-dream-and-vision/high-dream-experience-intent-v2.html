#0Ground

A place to work, not an app to manage

The product should feel like a professional knowledge-work environment whose specialist surfaces remain different entrances into one living research world.

Design intentAspirational / no implementation ceiling12 Aug 2026

The intended reality

Aspirational design intent for the complete knowledge-work environment.

This account describes the product as it should feel when it is fully itself. It treats current implementation as evidence, not as a ceiling. Its main subject is the lived work of a researcher inside one coherent environment.

The product should feel like a serious desktop instrument for long-form knowledge work. It can borrow useful interaction qualities from an IDE, a DAW, a visual canvas, a geographic cockpit, a non-linear editor, and an inhabitable spatial environment.

Those comparisons are not branding metaphors. They identify useful qualities of interaction. The software should remain one thing rather than become a bundle of borrowed applications.

Governing intent

The user should feel that they are moving around their work, not switching between unrelated research features.

Repository design lineageCurrent and historical design material used as evidence for this aspirational account.

A workspace before a dashboard

The product earns trust by returning the user to thought with almost no ceremony.

The application should open into useful work. It should not begin with a marketing-style home screen or feature grid when the user already has an active project.

A project is grounded in a real folder. A workspace is the active arrangement of that project: open tabs, selected constellations, views, edits, viewport positions, agent threads, and other state that matters to flow.

Last-session restore should therefore be normal. A user who stopped with a constellation, a timeline aperture, and a Journey draft open should return to those working contexts.

New project creation should remain short. The user chooses or creates a folder, sets the source horizon, and begins. The operating-system file picker remains useful because the filesystem is already part of the user’s mental model.

Simple when viewed, powerful when touched

The stage belongs to the work. Persistent chrome should stay small, stable, and legible. Advanced capability should live mainly in menus, right-click actions, keyboard shortcuts, command search, direct manipulation, and contextual inspectors.

This is the interaction grammar of mature professional software. The interface does not need to advertise every action at all times. Frequent use makes discoverability cumulative.

Modals should be rare because they interrupt spatial continuity. Use them for genuine transactional boundaries, destructive decisions, or compact focused tasks. Ordinary navigation, editing, filtering, and linking should usually happen in place.

The result should feel calm rather than sparse. There is a difference between hiding capability and placing capability where the current act makes it relevant.

Interaction law

Complexity belongs behind fluency. Expert power should emerge from context instead of occupying the screen permanently.

One world, many projections

The product is one research world that can be unfolded through several specialist modes.

The deepest promise is that the research exists once. Canvas, Timeline, Places, Journeys, and Palace are transformations of a common knowledge world.

A node edited on Canvas remains the node seen in Timeline. A location added in Places enriches the same object. A Journey scene can point to live node content. A Palace fixture can represent the same constellation at another scale.

This shared identity prevents content entropy. Work becomes more articulated as it moves through the product, but it does not become a stack of incompatible copies.

The user should trust that changing perspective does not silently change ontology. Each surface reveals different relations and enables different acts, while the underlying objects remain coherent.

Safety, reversibility, and sovereignty

The environment should invite provisional thought. Autosave should be quiet and reliable. History should make meaningful states recoverable without forcing manual version management after every change.

A researcher should be able to rearrange a constellation, alter a relationship, edit a View, or experiment with a Journey without wondering whether the previous useful state has vanished.

Local-first operation adds another layer of trust. Source files remain ordinary files. The research world does not depend on a hosted account.

Network use becomes an explicit membrane for search, live layers, external agents, publication, or collaboration rather than the condition of authorship.

Confidence condition

Work should feel reversible enough to encourage thought, and local enough to remain the user’s world.

The high dream

The ideal product is a sovereign knowledge-work environment in which a body of research becomes a living, recursive world. The user can unfold that world as conceptual space, historical time, geography, narrative sequence, or inhabitable architecture.

The user can work directly in every projection and return later without losing the thread. An intelligent agent can understand the same project state, use the same meaningful actions, and help without becoming the centre of the interface.

The product becomes valuable when it lets a researcher remain inside one coherent act of understanding while changing mode, scale, and medium as the work demands.

I have a body of work. I can enter it at different scales and through different modes, work directly upon it, and have an intelligent collaborator that understands the same world.
#1Definition

The things the user actually handles

Projects, workspaces, constellations, nodes, relationships, Views, Journeys, assets, and scope form a coherent manipulable world.

Project, workspace, and session

A project is the durable research scope. It is grounded in a folder and associated with the knowledge objects, source horizon, profiles, agent instructions, and saved work that make the project intelligible.

A workspace is a resumable arrangement of the project in use. It can contain open tabs, current surfaces, selected objects, temporary or saved Views, active agent threads, and viewport positions.

An application session is the current runtime. It should normally reopen a useful workspace rather than create a blank state.

This separation lets the filesystem remain simple while the application remembers richer flow.

ObjectMeaningContinuity
ProjectDurable research scope grounded in a folder.Long-lived.
WorkspaceResumable arrangement of the project in active use.Saved and reopenable.
SessionThe current running instance.Restores a workspace.

Constellations as recursive research objects

A constellation is a bounded but living articulation of the research. It can express a concept, inquiry, episode, historical situation, argument, person, place, work, document structure, or another meaningful local world.

A constellation does not need to be complete. It can begin as a dyad, a rough cluster, or an unfinished map and gain internal structure over time.

A sufficiently rich node can be entered as a constellation, while a constellation can be compressed into a node inside a larger constellation. The same research object can therefore move between object scale and world scale without duplication.

This recursive relation gives a common basis for nested histories, project subworlds, Palace architecture, and cross-project reuse.

Nodes, relationships, and assets

Nodes need several depths of encounter. At the lightest level, a node can be a title, visual mark, tag, or small image. Opened further, the same node can become a substantial document with rich text, Markdown, HTML, media, source links, and structured properties.

Relationships should be equally first-class. An edge can begin as a simple line or human note and later gain type, direction, evidence, temporal bounds, provenance, uncertainty, or richer interpretation.

The system should not require formal ontology before insight. It should permit a gesture first, then let the user or agent articulate that gesture into a more exact relation when useful.

Images, audio, video, documents, generated media, and external references are research material. They should not become foreign attachments managed in an unrelated subsystem.

Authoring rule

Structured knowledge should be derivable from natural work rather than requiring natural work to resemble database administration.

Views, Journeys, and durable interpretations

A carefully composed View is meaningful work. A Timeline aperture that includes particular nodes, tags, relations, and nested contexts expresses an interpretation.

Views should therefore be savable, nameable, reopenable, and reusable. They are more than viewport state, yet they should not be mistaken for copies of the underlying graph.

Journeys are a stronger authored form. They arrange scenes through knowledge and media, but they can still reference live underlying material. Palace layouts or curated Palace states may also deserve durable identity.

Several of these objects begin to behave like subprojects because they carry intention, local state, relevant objects, agent conversations, versions, and a point of resumption.

Strong project focus, free knowledge scope

The normal user experience should be strongly project-focused. Current files, saved Views, constellations, agent threads, and surface state should make it clear what body of work is active.

The graph itself can remain freer. A constellation may be relevant to several projects. A prior structure may be brought into a new project by relation rather than copy.

This requires explicit retrieval scope. The user or agent should be able to search within the selected object, current constellation, current project, selected projects, related projects, or the wider constellation bank.

Project boundaries control attention and context. They do not need to imply exclusive ownership of every knowledge object.

strong working boundary ≠ exclusive knowledge ownership

A single manipulable world

The product should expose one manipulable world built from recursive research objects, relationships, sources, media, Views, compositions, and resumable work state.

Different surfaces can privilege different parts of that world without inventing different data ownership. Project scope can remain legible while knowledge remains reusable.

Live references can remain distinct from snapshots. Derived presentation can remain distinct from canonical research.

These distinctions are technical hints, but they arise directly from the desired experience. They are therefore part of the product definition rather than implementation trivia.

#2Operation

Working through the surfaces

Each surface should have a distinct professional interaction grammar while preserving authorship over the same underlying research world.

The IDE/DAW shell

A calm technical workspace provides the continuity from which every surface can become more specialised.

The shell should be familiar enough that an experienced computer user understands it without explanation. Projects live in the filesystem. Workspaces restore. Tabs preserve local contexts. Libraries expose constellations, Views, Journeys, and sources.

Menus, context menus, commands, and keybindings carry most expert actions. Inspectors appear when an object needs deeper property editing. The agent remains summonable without occupying a permanent chat column.

The shell should make specialist surfaces feel like modes of one professional instrument. Switching to Timeline or Places should recompose the available controls rather than open another application inside the application.

Canvas: expressive construction first

Canvas should feel fast, safe, and playful enough for thinking in motion. The user should be able to create nodes, type, draw, connect, colour, tag, group, paste, drop assets, and introduce other constellations without leaving the spatial field.

A rough relationship can exist before it is formal. An edge can carry a note such as “possible institutional continuation.” The user or agent can later turn that note into one or more structured relationships.

Deep node content should remain close. A compact card can expand into substantial text and media. Rich properties should remain available through inspection or expert modes.

Autosave and recoverable history matter here because Canvas should encourage experimentation. The user should be able to make a mess, find the pattern, and formalise only what deserves formalisation.

Timeline: mapping by aperture

Timeline should be a working projection of the constellation. It can start from a full field or an empty field. The researcher constructs a useful historical aperture by including and excluding nodes, tags, relation types, nested contexts, historical registers, and trans-historical patterns.

Selecting a node should light its relational neighbourhood without flooding the view. The user can add another node, deepen relation depth, compare fields, or remove a branch.

Nested timelines give recursion practical force. Enter a person to inspect a life, a place to inspect local history, an event to inspect causes and consequences, or a work to inspect emergence and reception.

Saved Timeline Views become durable interpretations. As the underlying graph gains better dates, relationships, and evidence, those Views should normally benefit from the improvement.

Places: an immersive geographic cockpit

Places should be visually confident enough to feel like a high-end globe application. Geography should occupy almost the whole stage. Controls should stay minimal until the user asks for layers, history, search, or authoring tools.

The current project, constellation, saved View, or Journey defines the aperture. The researcher can follow people, events, routes, institutions, sources, media, movements, and recurring patterns through changing place and time.

The map can situate research inside relevant world information. Historical boundaries, weather, trade, transport, terrain, live travel conditions, imagery, and other layers can become explanatory context when the research requires them.

This is also an authoring surface. The user can edit a place, create a route, refine a temporal location, add media, or introduce a new research object without returning to Canvas.

Journeys: compose movement through knowledge

Journeys should borrow the clearest grammar from non-linear editors without becoming a full video workstation. The user needs scenes, tracks, ordering, duration, preview, rearrangement, media placement, narration, music, and simple export.

A Journey can begin from a constellation, Timeline View, geographic path, selected node set, or another useful aperture. Its scenes can combine text, quotation, images, video, audio, map states, timeline states, narration, generated visuals, or other media.

The key architectural behaviour is live reference. If a scene points to the current image or selected text of a node, a later edit should normally propagate. The user can deliberately snapshot or override content when editorial stability requires it.

Rendered outputs can return to the knowledge world as media attached to the node or constellation they explain.

Palace: traverse the shape of the whole

The Palace should represent the project at a scale that other Views cannot. It should feel like an inhabitable house of knowledge whose architecture emerges from the project’s real constellation structure and curation.

A constellation should not become a room only because the renderer needs boxes. Its role and scale can justify a room, corridor, wing, stair, gallery, courtyard, tower, object, wall, library, or nested structure.

The researcher must recognise the work directly. Images, titles, diagrams, media, collections, symbols, Journey artifacts, and constellation structures should inhabit the environment.

Navigation should support several scales. The user can walk, click, inspect, enter, fly outward, and examine larger architectural relations. The Palace becomes valuable when learning its spatial form teaches something true about the project.

#3Pattern

The pattern beneath the views

One object with many projections, recursive scale, authored apertures, live reference, recoverable history, and shared actions keep the product coherent.

Research once; transform the view

The same canonical object should be available to every surface that can make meaningful use of it. A surface may derive layout, filtering, scene composition, camera state, or display metadata, but it should not silently fork the underlying research.

The law does not forbid derived artifacts. It requires every derived artifact to know whether it references, snapshots, transforms, or exports something else.

Editing a date can improve Timeline and Places. Updating an image can improve Canvas, Journey, and Palace. Adding evidence can strengthen the same relation wherever it appears.

Product law

Research once; transform the view, not the knowledge.

Anything can become a world

The product wants a recursive object model. A thing can be simple enough to manipulate as one node and rich enough to enter as an interior constellation.

Entering unfolds an object into its meaningful interior. Leaving compresses that interior back into a unit that can participate in a larger structure.

The same pattern can support nested timelines, project subworlds, Palace rooms, archived conversations, and media packages without claiming that all of those things are identical.

The architectural task is to preserve recursion while allowing each domain object to retain its own role and rules.

Anything sufficiently rich can be entered. Anything entered can be re-grounded as an object within a larger world.

Aperture is a form of authorship

Research often advances by deciding what belongs in the current field. The user may begin with everything and subtract, or begin with nothing and include only what is relevant.

This pattern appears on Timeline and Places, but it also applies to Canvas Views, agent retrieval scope, Journeys, and Palace curation.

Because an aperture can embody an interpretation, the product should let users save and name it. A saved View should be able to become the input to another surface instead of becoming a screenshot.

Aperture protects attention. The graph can remain broad while the working field stays intentionally small.

whole project − deliberate exclusions + selected context = working aperture

Live reference and deliberate snapshot

The product needs an explicit distinction between a reference and a copy. A Journey scene can display current node content. A Palace object can display the current title or image. A saved View can query the current graph.

Those relationships should remain live by default when the user has chosen a semantic reference. Propagation is then a feature rather than accidental coupling.

Sometimes the user needs a fixed state. Publication, historical comparison, or deliberate editorial divergence may require a snapshot or override. That should be an explicit act with visible provenance.

For any derived presentation, the user should be able to answer a simple question: does this follow its source, or is it intentionally frozen?

ModeBehaviourUse
Live referenceFollows the selected source field or object.Living research compositions.
SnapshotCaptures a known state.Publication, comparison, archival checkpoints.
OverrideForks selected presentation content while retaining provenance.Editorial adaptation.

History without manual version ceremony

The user should not manage ordinary research like a software release branch. The system can maintain object history, workspace checkpoints, and meaningful version boundaries automatically.

A constellation may need to return to an earlier structure. A saved View may need comparison with a later one. A Journey may need editorial versions. Workspace restoration may need a short history of useful states.

The user-facing rule should stay consistent even if mechanisms differ by object type: important changes are recoverable, named versions are possible, and ordinary work does not demand constant version administration.

This history also makes agent work safer because automated transformations become inspectable and reversible rather than opaque mutations.

Surface parity through shared actions

Important product capabilities should exist as stable semantic actions. Create node, relate objects, add source, save View, open constellation, include in timeline, attach to scene, place geographically, and export are examples.

A human may trigger an action by drag, menu, command, keybinding, or chat. An agent may trigger the same action through an agent-native capability layer.

This avoids a split architecture in which humans use the application while agents operate a separate hidden database.

Surface parity does not mean identical controls. It means equal access to meaningful underlying acts when the current surface can support them.

#4Context

Intelligence and context without intrusion

Filesystem grounding, live world layers, cross-project retrieval, conversations, and agent harnesses should expand the research world without dissolving its boundaries.

Filesystem-grounded source horizon

Project creation should establish a folder as the default source horizon. The application can index compatible files and subfolders automatically while leaving the originals in place.

The user should be able to include or exclude branches through a simple folder checklist. Generated directories, private material, or irrelevant archives can stay outside the active horizon without relocation.

Source acquisition should preserve provenance. Derived text, extracted metadata, embeddings, graph nodes, transcripts, thumbnails, or other working artifacts should remain distinguishable from the canonical source files that produced them.

This keeps the project compatible with Finder, shell tools, version control, backup systems, editors, and future software.

LayerResponsibility
FilesystemCanonical local files and ordinary user ownership.
Source horizonProject-relative inclusion and exclusion of material available for research.
Derived research stateIndexing, extraction, graph structure, provenance, curation, Views and generated artifacts.

An intelligent collaborator native to the app

The agent should understand the active project, workspace, surface, selected object, current constellation, current View, available actions, and relevant project instructions. The user should not need to restate that context in every prompt.

Natural language should be able to operate the product. The user can ask the agent to extract relationships from sources, research supporting material, build a Timeline aperture, place objects geographically, organise a Canvas, create a Journey draft, or explain Palace structure.

The useful model is agent-native action rather than chat-assisted software. The application exposes meaningful capabilities, and the agent uses those capabilities with the same domain semantics available to the human.

The agent should remain available without becoming visual furniture. A compact conversational surface can sit above a more open terminal or session substrate.

Conversations become part of research without becoming the ontology

Projects should have conversations, and conversations should attach naturally to a constellation, node, Journey, investigation, media artifact, or other working context.

A user can reopen a thread and continue thought from where it left off. The thread carries episodic metadata such as time, project, related objects, agent harness, and important actions.

Useful results can be condensed into durable objects. A conversation might produce a new constellation, several relationships, a supporting note, a saved View, or an unresolved question.

The raw conversation remains available as provenance and process trace. Chat remains a natural exploratory medium without making the knowledge world a pile of transcripts.

Conversation law

Conversation can become knowledge without pretending that conversation itself is the final knowledge object.

Live world context as an opt-in research layer

External live information is most powerful when it is situated against a research question rather than presented as a generic feed. Places provides the clearest example because weather, transport, trade, borders, terrain, and imagery can change interpretation.

The same principle can extend elsewhere. A current source search can support a node. A live database can enrich an institution. A recent archive update can widen a source horizon.

Local-first does not mean disconnected. It means network use is a visible capability with a known purpose and trust boundary.

The user should be able to distinguish local canonical material from fetched context and generated interpretation.

Familiar agent experience, open harness underneath

The user-facing agent experience should be stable and polished. Underneath, the application should remain able to ride terminal-native or API-native harnesses such as Claude Code, Codex, Pi, local systems, or future agents.

Project-level configuration should resemble the useful parts of repository-native agent setup. A base application protocol teaches the agent what the product is, which actions exist, how provenance works, and how scope should be handled.

Project instructions then add domain vocabulary, preferred sources, research constraints, skills, and workflows.

The durable contract becomes the application action and context interface rather than one vendor protocol.

Human judgement stays consequential

The agent can carry broad research, extraction, organisation, transformation, and repetitive execution. The human remains the source of consequential authorship, interpretive commitment, and recognition.

This does not require constant approval prompts. Reversible operations can often proceed directly when the user has delegated them. More consequential changes should remain inspectable, attributable, and easy to refine or reverse.

The application should make agent contributions legible in the same world where human work appears. Provenance, change history, proposal state, and object-level traces can provide confidence without creating a second supervisory interface.

The desired experience is intelligence without intrusion. The agent is close when wanted, powerful when asked, and quiet when the user is directly working.

Authority boundary

Human attention belongs primarily in vision, interpretation, authorship, and judgement. The agent should carry the developmental body of delegated work.

#5Synthesis

The whole experience

Professional workspace behaviour, recursive knowledge, surface-specific authorship, local sovereignty, and agent-native intelligence should reinforce one another.

A day inside the environment

The application opens into the last workspace. A constellation, historical View, and unfinished Journey are already where the user left them. New source files added to the project folder are available for inspection and indexing.

The user opens Canvas and roughs out several relationships. One edge remains provisional. The agent reads a source set and proposes supporting evidence. The user keeps one relationship, rewrites another, and opens a person as a nested constellation.

Timeline reframes the same work historically. The user starts from a saved aperture, adds the person’s local timeline, and removes unrelated branches. Places shows the selected movement against changing borders and period transport context.

A focused View becomes a Journey. Existing node text, images, map states, and narration are arranged into scenes. A later node-image edit propagates to the live scene.

Before stopping, the user flies through the Palace at project scale, notices an architectural gap corresponding to an underdeveloped constellation, and starts an agent thread around it. The workspace returns next time.

The governing experiential laws

The product can remain coherent across considerable technical freedom if a small set of experiential laws stay stable. These are not feature requirements. They are tests for whether a feature belongs in the product in the first place.

The stage belongs to the work.Chrome recedes until the current act needs it.
Folders ground projects; projects do not imprison knowledge.Filesystem familiarity and graph freedom coexist.
Work restores.A workspace should reopen where thought was left.
Manipulate first; formalise when useful.Structure can emerge from expressive work instead of blocking it.
Every surface remains an authoring surface.Changing viewpoint does not reduce the researcher to a spectator.
One object, many projections.Content does not fork merely because the mode changes.
Views are interpretations, not screenshots.Apertures through the graph are meaningful, reusable work.
Scale is traversable.A node may open into a world; a world may become an object.
Agents use the application.They act through shared domain capabilities rather than a parallel hidden ontology.
Complexity hides behind fluency.Expert depth appears through context, commands, menus, gestures and keybindings.

The emotional character of expert use

Continuity matters first. The user can leave and return without reconstructing context. The environment remembers the arrangement of work without turning that memory into ceremony.

Safety follows. Autosave, history, provenance, and reversible operations make experimentation cheap enough to support real thought. Directness means the user can usually act on the thing they can see.

Depth should remain discoverable beneath calm surfaces. Spatial confidence should make project, constellation, View, place, timeline, Journey, and Palace context legible. Scale should feel fluid rather than rigid.

Sovereignty comes from local ownership and ordinary files. Seriousness comes from expecting long-term expert use. Play remains important because mapping, traversing, composing, and discovering patterns should be enjoyable.

The agent adds a final quality: intelligence without intrusion. The user can ask for breadth and speed without losing the tactile sense of authorship.

What the product must not collapse into

It is not a Miro clone with an AI sidebar. It is not a graph database browser with alternate renderers. It is not a GIS package with notes attached. It is not a timeline visualiser, a Notion clone, a 3D knowledge toy, a video editor, or an AI chat application.

Each of those categories can contribute a mature interaction pattern. None should define the whole because each would make the other surfaces secondary integrations.

The product should not become a generic everything app. Its coherence comes from one strong subject: forming, navigating, contextualising, composing, and revisiting a body of research through a common knowledge world.

The correct test is whether a new capability deepens that world or merely expands the feature inventory.

Boundary test

A feature belongs when it makes the common research world easier to form, inspect, traverse, contextualise, express, or remember.

Technical primitives implied by the experience

This account intentionally stops before locking the architecture. However, the experiential requirements repeatedly point toward a small set of primitives or contracts that deserve explicit contemplation next.

These are not implementation tasks yet. They are pressure points. If the architecture cannot express them cleanly, the user experience will eventually fragment even if each surface appears polished.

The house of knowledge under construction

The product begins with ordinary materials and provisional ideas. Those materials become interconnected objects. Objects gather interiority. Constellations arise. Constellations become historical and geographical. Selected movements become Journeys. Developed expression returns as media. The accumulated body becomes architecture.

At every point, the human can act directly. At every point, the agent can work on the same visible world. At every point, the underlying research remains available rather than being consumed by its current presentation.

The ideal experience is not “I am switching between several research tools.” The ideal experience is “I am moving around my work.” Sometimes the user looks from above. Sometimes the user enters one thing. Sometimes the user follows it through time, crosses its geography, composes a path, or learns the structure that the whole has made.

That is the experience the later primitive design, system architecture, development program, and product HTML should protect.

A house of knowledge under active construction: local, recursive, manipulable, traversable, expressive, and intelligent.
